# Voice profile (example)

Copy this to `HUMANIZE_STYLE.md` in your project root and edit it. The skill loads it
automatically and rewrites in this voice instead of the built-in default.

Keep it short. Rules the skill can actually check beat adjectives it has to interpret.

## Tone

- Direct and specific. Take a position instead of surveying both sides.
- Confident, not salesy. No hype adjectives.

## Mechanics

- Contractions: yes.
- Em dashes: no. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- Person: first person plural ("we") for company copy, second person ("you") for docs.
- Headings: sentence case.
- Oxford comma: yes.

## Never

- Open with a rhetorical question.
- Close with "What are your thoughts?" or any engagement bait.
- Use "leverage", "utilize", "seamless", or "robust".

## Always

- Lead with a concrete number, name, or example where one exists.
- Vary sentence length. Follow a long sentence with a short one.
